## 1. Proprietary AI Platforms

These are closed or commercial systems accessible through **APIs, subscriptions, or cloud-based interfaces**.  
They deliver professional-grade AI capabilities but do not release their full model weights or architectures.

### ⚙️ Major Proprietary Providers

- **OpenAI** — GPT-4o, ChatGPT, DALL·E 3, Whisper, and Sora (text-to-video).  
  🔗 [https://openai.com](https://openai.com)

- **Anthropic** — The **Claude** model family, emphasizing reliability, reasoning, and constitutional AI.  
  🔗 [https://www.anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com)

- **Google DeepMind** — **Gemini**, **Imagen**, and related multimodal research models.  
  🔗 [https://deepmind.google](https://deepmind.google)

- **Amazon Bedrock / AWS AI** — Enterprise-scale hosting for third-party models and APIs.  
  🔗 [https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock)

- **Microsoft Azure AI** — Enterprise access to OpenAI models and cognitive services.  
  🔗 [https://azure.microsoft.com](https://azure.microsoft.com)

- **IBM WatsonX** — A business-oriented AI and data platform with tools for governance and analytics.  
  🔗 [https://www.ibm.com/watsonx](https://www.ibm.com/watsonx)

### 🎨 Creative Proprietary AI Tools

- **Midjourney** — [https://www.midjourney.com](https://www.midjourney.com)  
  Text-to-image generation through Discord; produces richly stylized, high-fidelity imagery for concept art and design.

- **Runway ML** — [https://runwayml.com](https://runwayml.com)  
  Professional AI-powered video-editing and generation platform used in film and creative industries.

- **Adobe Firefly** — [https://www.adobe.com/sensei/generative-ai/firefly.html](https://www.adobe.com/sensei/generative-ai/firefly.html)  
  Integrates generative tools into Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere Pro; trained on licensed data for commercial safety.

- **Stability AI DreamStudio** — [https://dreamstudio.ai](https://dreamstudio.ai)  
  Hosted version of Stable Diffusion with prompt weighting, fine-tuning, and commercial usage options.

## 2. Open-Source / Freely Served AI Platforms

These ecosystems enable researchers and creators to **download, fine-tune, or self-host models**, often under open licenses.

### 🤗 Hugging Face — [https://huggingface.co](https://huggingface.co)

Community hub for open-source models, datasets, and demo “Spaces.”  
The de facto *GitHub for AI* where users collaborate on training and deployment.

### 🧩 Ollama — [https://ollama.com](https://ollama.com)

Run LLMs such as Llama 3, Mistral, or Phi 3 **locally** on your machine.  
Perfect for private, offline experimentation and integration with LangChain or Open WebUI.

### ☁️ Replicate — [https://replicate.com](https://replicate.com)

Cloud service for running and hosting open models via simple APIs.  
Ideal for developers who want scalable inference without managing hardware.

### ⚡ Fal.ai — [https://fal.ai](https://fal.ai)

Low-latency inference platform for diffusion and language models, offering flexible APIs for creative deployment.

## 4. Experimental and Independent Projects

### 🧠 Realtime Transformer by Marlon Barrios Solano  
🔗 [https://realtimetransformer-r267.vercel.app/](https://realtimetransformer-r267.vercel.app/)

An interactive web application created by **Marlon Barrios Solano** that allows users to **customize the system prompt** of an AI model in real time.  
It explores live AI interaction, performative text transformation, and the boundaries between **prompt engineering, creative coding, and generative performance**.  
This tool functions both as an educational experiment and as an artistic interface for understanding **prompt dynamics** in LLMs.
